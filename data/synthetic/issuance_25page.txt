### Article 2.2 — Market Abuse Regulation Compliance

The Issuer is subject to UK Market Abuse Regulation ("UK MAR"), as retained under the European
Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018. The Issuer must not engage in insider dealing, market manipulation,

# PART IV — INDENTURE — KEY TERMS SUMMARY


### Article 4.1 — Covenants Summary

The Notes shall be issued pursuant to an Indenture dated as of the Issue Date between the Issuer,
the Guarantors, and the Trustee, which shall contain the following material restrictive covenants
(each subject to carve-outs, exceptions, and baskets to be agreed in the Indenture):

**Limitation on Indebtedness**: The Issuer and its restricted subsidiaries shall not incur
additional indebtedness unless, after giving pro forma effect to such incurrence, the Consolidated
Net Leverage Ratio does not exceed 5.0x EBITDA (the "Leverage Ratio Test"), or unless such
indebtedness falls within a permitted basket (including a Permitted Debt basket of GBP 50 million
and a Revolving Credit Facility basket of GBP 100 million);

**Limitation on Restricted Payments**: The Issuer shall not make dividends, distributions,
purchases of equity, payments to affiliates, or other Restricted Payments unless the conditions set
out in the Indenture are met, including a Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio of at least 2.0x;

**Limitation on Liens**: The Issuer shall not create or permit to exist any lien on its assets
other than Permitted Liens as defined in the Indenture, including the first ranking security
created in connection with the Notes, the revolving credit facility security, and purchase money
liens incurred in the ordinary course of business;

**Limitation on Asset Sales**: The Issuer shall not sell, transfer, lease, or otherwise dispose of
any assets outside the ordinary course of business unless (i) the consideration is at least equal to
fair market value as certified by the Board of Directors, (ii) at least 75% of the consideration is
cash or Cash Equivalents, and (iii) Excess Proceeds above GBP 20 million in any twelve-month period
are applied to repurchase Notes at par;

**Change of Control**: Upon a Change of Control (as defined in the Indenture), the Issuer must
offer to repurchase all outstanding Notes at a price equal to 101% of par plus accrued and unpaid
interest to the repurchase date.


### Article 4.2 — Events of Default

The Indenture shall provide that each of the following events constitutes an Event of Default:

(a) Failure to pay interest on the Notes when due, and continuation of such failure for a period of
30 days;

(b) Failure to pay principal on any Note when due at maturity, upon redemption, upon required
repurchase, upon declaration, or otherwise;

(c) Failure to comply with any covenant or agreement under the Indenture (other than those relating
to payment) and continuation of such failure for a cure period of 60 days after written notice from
the Trustee or Holders of 25% or more of the outstanding Notes;

(d) Any representation or warranty in the Indenture or any security document proving to have been
inaccurate in any material respect when made, and continuation of such inaccuracy for 30 days after
the Issuer obtains knowledge thereof;

(e) Any default by the Issuer or any Material Subsidiary in payments due under other indebtedness
exceeding GBP 25 million (cross-default), or the acceleration of such indebtedness;

(f) Insolvency of the Issuer or any Material Subsidiary, including the commencement of
administration, liquidation, or receivership proceedings;

(g) Invalidity or unenforceability of any security document over assets representing more than 15%
of Consolidated Total Assets, which is not remedied within 30 days of written notice from the
Trustee.

Upon the occurrence and continuance of an Event of Default, the Trustee (or the Required Holders)
may, by written notice to the Issuer, declare all outstanding principal and accrued interest to be
immediately due and payable. The Issuer shall notify the Trustee within two Business Days of
obtaining knowledge of any default or Event of Default.
