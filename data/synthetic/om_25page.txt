# PART I — SUMMARY OF OFFERING


### Article 1.1 — The Offering

Harrow Street Capital Fund III LP (the "Fund") is a Delaware limited partnership formed on January
10, 2026, for the purpose of making investments primarily in private credit, structured equity, and
special situations transactions across North America and Western Europe. The Fund is being offered
exclusively to "accredited investors" as defined in Regulation D, Rule 501(a) under the Securities
Act of 1933, as amended (the "Securities Act"), and, where applicable, to "qualified purchasers"

set out in Section 3(c)(7) of that Act.

The General Partner, Harrow Street Capital GP III LLC (the "General Partner"), is a Delaware
limited liability company and is the sole general partner of the Fund. The General Partner and its
affiliates constitute registered investment advisers under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (the
"Advisers Act") and are regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC"). By accepting
delivery of this Offering Memorandum, each prospective investor acknowledges that no regulatory
authority has confirmed the accuracy or adequacy of this document and that any representation to
the contrary is an offence.

The Fund is being offered in a private placement exempt from registration under Section 4(a)(2) of
the Securities Act and Rule 506(b) promulgated thereunder. Interests in the Fund (the "Interests")
have not been registered under the Securities Act or the securities laws of any state or other
jurisdiction, and may not be offered or sold except in transactions that are exempt from the
registration requirements of the Securities Act and any applicable state securities laws. Each
investor shall be required to make representations confirming their accredited investor and, where

# PART II — INVESTMENT STRATEGY AND RESTRICTIONS


### Article 2.1 — Investment Objective and Strategy

The Fund's investment objective is to generate risk-adjusted returns superior to comparable public
market indices by investing in privately negotiated transactions where the General Partner believes
value can be created through active asset management, financial restructuring, or operational
improvement. The Fund will target an investment portfolio of 15–25 assets diversified across
industry sectors, geographies, and risk profiles.

The Fund shall pursue the following sub-strategies:

**Private Credit**: Senior secured and unitranche loans, second lien loans, and mezzanine debt
instruments arranged on a direct lending basis to middle-market and upper-middle-market borrowers
with EBITDA between USD 20 million and USD 150 million. Private credit investments shall represent
at least 30% of invested capital during the Investment Period.

**Structured Equity**: Preferred equity, convertible notes, revenue participations, and other
hybrid instruments combining debt-like downside protection with equity upside, primarily in growth-
stage and mature private companies. Structured equity investments shall represent up to 40% of
invested capital.

**Special Situations**: Opportunistic credit including distressed debt purchases, post-
reorganization equity, rescue financing, and situations arising from regulatory catalysts, sector
dislocation, or financing market disruption. Special situations shall represent up to 30% of
invested capital at cost.


### Article 2.2 — Investment Restrictions and Concentration Limits

The General Partner shall observe the following investment restrictions in managing the Portfolio,
which may be amended only with the approval of a majority of the Advisory Committee:

(a) No single Portfolio Company shall represent more than 15% of aggregate capital commitments at
cost;

(b) Investments in Portfolio Companies domiciled outside the United States and Canada shall not in
aggregate exceed 25% of aggregate capital commitments at cost;

(c) The Fund shall not acquire majority control of a Portfolio Company without first obtaining a
fairness opinion from a qualified independent financial adviser where the investment exceeds USD 50
million;

(d) Leverage at the Fund level (facilities other than individual Portfolio Company financings) shall
not exceed 30% of the net asset value of the Portfolio as of the most recent quarterly valuation
date, and shall be limited to bridge facilities that are repaid within 180 days of drawdown;

(e) The Fund shall not invest in publicly traded securities as a primary strategy, although the Fund
may hold publicly traded securities received as consideration in a restructuring, merger or
disposition of a Portfolio Company investment for up to 18 months following receipt;

(f) Investments in companies in the cannabis, tobacco, weapons manufacturing, or adult entertainment
industries are prohibited, in accordance with the Fund's Environmental, Social and Governance
("ESG") Policy adopted on the Fund's formation date.


# PART III — RISK FACTORS


### Article 3.1 — General Investment Risks

Investment in the Fund involves a substantial degree of risk, including the risk of loss of the
entire amount invested. Prospective investors should carefully consider the following risk factors
and all other information in this Offering Memorandum before making an investment decision. The
following risk factors are not exhaustive, and additional risks not discussed herein may also
adversely affect the Fund's performance.

**Illiquidity**: Interests in the Fund are illiquid and subject to long-term restrictions on
transfer. There is no public market for Interests, and none is expected to develop. Limited Partners
wishing to transfer their Interests must obtain the prior written consent of the General Partner,
which may be withheld in its sole discretion. Limited Partners should assume they will be unable
to liquidate their investment in the Fund for the full term of the Fund.

**Manager Dependence**: The performance of the Fund is substantially dependent on the skill and
expertise of key investment professionals. The loss of key persons, including the principal
investment professionals of the General Partner, could have a material adverse effect on the
performance of the Fund. The LPA contains a Key Person provision pursuant to which the Investment
Period shall be suspended if fewer than two of the designated Key Persons remain actively involved
in the management of the Fund.

**Valuation Uncertainty**: Private investments are difficult to value accurately because they are
not traded on public markets. The General Partner shall establish and maintain a written valuation
policy for the Fund in accordance with applicable GAAP standards and SEC guidance for registered
investment advisers. The valuation policy shall require that all material investments be subject to
an independent valuation review at least annually by an independent, qualified valuation firm.

**Leverage Risk**: Portfolio Company investments may involve the use of significant financial
leverage, which increases both the potential for return and the risk of loss. In the event of a
decline in the value of a Portfolio Company's assets, leverage may magnify losses and could result
in the total loss of the Fund's investment.

**Regulatory and Compliance Risk**: Changes in applicable laws, including securities laws,
regulatory capital requirements, banking regulations, tax laws and tax treaties could adversely
affect the Fund's ability to implement its investment strategy or result in unanticipated costs or
losses. The Fund shall maintain a compliance programme designed to monitor and manage material
regulatory risk, including appointment of a Chief Compliance Officer for the General Partner.

**Counterparty Risk**: The Fund's investments will generally be undocumented or privately
negotiated, and the Fund will rely on the creditworthiness of counterparties in connection with
trading and investment activities. The failure of any counterparty to fulfil its obligations could
result in significant losses.


### Article 3.2 — Specific Risk Disclosures

**Credit Risk**: Investments in private credit and mezzanine instruments are exposed to the risk
that borrowers will fail to make timely payments of interest or principal, or that the value of
collateral securing any loan will be insufficient to cover outstanding obligations. The General
Partner must disclose to prospective investors all material credit risks associated with any
investment class constituting more than 10% of the Fund's portfolio.

**Market Risk**: Valuations of Portfolio Company investments are exposed to changes in macroeconomic
conditions, including interest rate movements, credit spread widening, currency fluctuations, and
equity market dislocations. The Fund does not systematically hedge market risk at the Fund level.
Investors should be aware that market risk exposures may be correlated across multiple Portfolio
Company investments.

**ERISA Considerations**: The General Partner shall monitor the "plan asset" status of the Fund on
an ongoing basis. If 25% or more of the value of any class of interests in the Fund is held by
"benefit plan investors" as defined under ERISA and the DOL's Plan Asset Regulation, the General
Partner and the Fund shall be subject to ERISA's fiduciary requirements and prohibited transaction
rules. The General Partner shall endeavour to maintain a benefit plan investor percentage below 25%
for each class of interests and shall provide quarterly notices of the current percentage to all
Limited Partners.

**Tax Risk**: Prospective investors are advised to consult their own tax advisers regarding the
applicable tax consequences of an investment in the Fund. Tax laws applicable to the Fund and its
investments may be subject to interpretation, legislative change, or adverse regulatory action that
could result in materially increased tax obligations for the Fund or for individual investors.

# PART VI — DISTRIBUTIONS AND WATERFALL


### Article 6.1 — Distribution Waterfall

All amounts available for distribution from the Fund (including proceeds from sales of Portfolio
Company investments, income, and other receipts) shall be distributed in the following order of
priority (the "Distribution Waterfall"):

First, 100% to all Limited Partners pro rata in proportion to their funded capital commitments,
until each Limited Partner has received an amount equal to 100% of its aggregate capital
contributions;

Second, 100% to all Limited Partners pro rata in proportion to their funded capital commitments,
until each Limited Partner has received a cumulative preferred return at the Hurdle Rate of 8% per
annum, compounded annually, on all contributed capital from the date of each drawdown to the date
of distribution (the "Preferred Return Catch-Up");

Third, 100% to the General Partner until the General Partner has received 20% of the sum of (i)
the total Preferred Return distributions made in the Second tier and (ii) distributions to the
General Partner in this Third tier (the "GP Catch-Up");

Fourth, 80% to all Limited Partners pro rata in proportion to their funded capital commitments and
20% to the General Partner (as Carried Interest).

The General Partner shall submit a distribution calculation to the Fund's auditors for confirmation
before each distribution to the extent such distribution includes Carried Interest, and shall
provide Limited Partners with a written distribution notice at least 10 Business Days before each
distribution, detailing the source of funds and the amount payable to each Limited Partner.


### Article 6.2 — Clawback Obligation

If, upon the final liquidation of the Fund, the General Partner has received cumulative Carried
Interest distributions in excess of the amounts that would have been available to it based on the
total realised returns of the Fund calculated in accordance with the Distribution Waterfall, the
General Partner shall repay such excess to the Fund within 90 days following the final distribution
date (the "Clawback Obligation"). The Clawback Obligation shall be secured by an escrow account
equal to 30% of cumulative Carried Interest distributions, which shall be maintained by a third-
party escrow agent and released to the General Partner only upon the written certification of the
Fund's auditors that no Clawback Obligation remains outstanding.

Individual principals of the General Partner shall provide personal guarantees of the Clawback
Obligation in proportion to their respective interests in the General Partner, secured by a pledge
of their interests in the General Partner. The General Partner shall provide Annual Clawback
Reporting to all Limited Partners showing the cumulative Carried Interest received, the cumulative
preferred return distributed, and the estimated Clawback Obligation as of the end of each fiscal
year.


# PART VII — ADVISORY COMMITTEE


### Article 7.1 — Composition and Role

The Advisory Committee shall comprise representatives of four to six Limited Partners chosen by the
General Partner from among the largest institutional investors in the Fund. Representation on the
Advisory Committee shall not confer fiduciary duties on Limited Partner representatives, and
Advisory Committee members shall not be responsible for investment decisions of the Fund. The
Advisory Committee shall meet at least twice per year, with at least one in-person meeting annually.

The Advisory Committee shall have the right to: (i) approve or disapprove potential conflicts of
interest or related-party transactions as described in Article 8 hereof; (ii) approve extensions of
the Investment Period or the Fund's term; (iii) approve any change to the Management Fee rate;
(iv) approve valuations of investments where a conflict of interest exists; and (v) remove the
General Partner for Cause (together with a two-thirds vote of Limited Partners).

The General Partner shall provide the Advisory Committee with advance written notice of any
transaction involving a potential conflict of interest at least 10 Business Days before the
anticipated transaction date. The Advisory Committee may request additional information within 5
Business Days, and the General Partner shall not proceed with the transaction without Advisory
Committee approval or the expiry of a 15 Business Day response period.

# SCHEDULE C — COVENANTS AND MONITORING OBLIGATIONS

### C.1 — Portfolio Company Covenants

The General Partner shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the following material
protective covenants in documentation governing each debt investment constituting more than 5% of
invested capital:

(a) **Financial Performance Covenant**: A maximum net leverage ratio tested quarterly (typically
